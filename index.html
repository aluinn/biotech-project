About

AlphaFold is an AI system built by Google DeepMind that predicts a protein's 3D shape from its sequence of amino acids. It was unveiled in 2020 and solved a puzzle biologists had worked on for 50 years. Its creators, Demis Hassabis and John Jumper, shared the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry. These are predictions, not experimentally determined structures, so treat them as a very good guide rather than the final word.
